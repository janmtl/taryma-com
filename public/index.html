Welcome

Ewa Taryma-Myslinski is a distinguished artist whose work spans multiple media including oil, pastel, coal, chalk, watercolors, and graphics. Explore her biography, exhibitions, and various studies inspired by the beautiful landscapes and people of North America and beyond.
