Posters

I was fortunate to be personally invited to study for over 5 years and graduate under one of the most famous Polish artists, F. Starowieyski ([2], [3], [4]). He was the first Polish artist to have a one man show at the Museum of Modern Art in New York in 1986. One of his artistic pursuits was posters. With these posters, I am following in his footsteps.
